# Derby Day stream brief: onboarding

You are one of several parallel Claude Code sessions improving "Derby Day" (repo zuzivian/derby-day, private), a zero-dependency TypeScript browser football game: a club manager where you also play every match yourself with the mouse, in the spirit of the 2005-2016 browser game Power Soccer. 3D via vendored three.js r186 in vendor/three; a deterministic 60 Hz sim in src/sim (pure: seeded RNG, no Math.random/Date/DOM, all state in MatchState); 11 v 11 under the Laws of the Game; every match 2 x 3 minutes; replays are input logs re-simulated (src/meta/replay.ts); two-tab lockstep multiplayer (src/net). It deploys to Vercel from `main` (derby.natwong.dev). Read README.md and docs/ROADMAP.md first, then the code you own.

YOUR WORKSTREAM: gradual onboarding. Owner ask 14 ("make the onboarding even more gradual get to the payoff of playing games asap, reveal more of the menus over time").
- First run: get the player into a real match within one or two clicks. No forms first: a single welcome with one big Kick off that starts their first match against an easy CPU club (club name and colours generated; let them rename later).
- Teach inside that first match with light, non-blocking coach tips (new src/client/coach.ts) triggered by situations: how to run (pointer distance), first time with the ball (click to pass), near goal (right click to shoot), losing the ball (right click to tackle), first restart. Each tip appears once, is dismissible, never pauses play for long, and is announced for screen readers. The separate training-ground drills become an optional "Practice skills" entry unlocked later, not a gate.
- After the first match: a short celebration of the result and XP, then offer to name the club and pick colours (skippable).
- Reveal menus over time: at level 1 the player sees only Home with Kick off (no Squad/Club/Ladder tabs yet); then reveal tabs and features one at a time with the level unlocks (e.g. Squad at level 2, Club at level 3, Training, Transfers, Ladder, Practice skills, Cup, League...). Adjust the UNLOCKS table in src/meta/levels.ts, keep its tests consistent, and introduce each newly revealed tab with a small coach mark/spotlight that points at it once.
